# Licensing in plain words

*This page is guidance. [LICENSE-APP.txt](LICENSE-APP.txt) is the licence and controls if the two differ. It is not legal advice.*

The Velora app is licensed under the **Business Source License 1.1** (BSL); the text is in [LICENSE-APP.txt](LICENSE-APP.txt). This
repository publishes the app as downloadable builds, with the website and documentation, not as source code. The app is **free to
use** in the ways below. Using it in production for a business needs a commercial licence from Zyvor AI Labs. The BSL says each
version becomes available under Apache-2.0 four years after it is first published.

## Free

- **Home and personal use**, including a home lab, your own Macs, and household use.
- **Learning, teaching and research**, including at schools, universities and non-profits.
- **Development and testing**, including CI, QA, demos and proofs of concept, **inside a company too**.
- **Labs and evaluation**: trying Velora out, benchmarking it, training on it, running it in a lab or staging environment that does
  not carry live business work.

## Needs a commercial licence

- Running Velora **in production for a business**: to carry live work for a company, serve its customers or users, or support
  revenue-generating operations.
- Anything else the licence does not allow. If you are unsure, ask before you deploy.

## Things people ask

- **Is a company's internal lab "production"?** A lab, test or staging setup that does not carry live business work is
  non-production. A shared team endpoint that people rely on for their daily work is production.
- **Do I get a licence to macOS?** No. macOS is licensed by Apple, separately: see [MACOS-LICENSING.md](MACOS-LICENSING.md).
- **What happens after four years?** The version you have then becomes available under Apache-2.0, as the licence says.
- **How do I get a commercial licence?** Contact Zyvor AI Labs (contact details to be added before release).
